The role

What you will be doing

You will be the consultant who intuitively understands what clients need, balances their immediate priorities with longer-term strategic goals, and applies your experience creatively to new and complex challenges.

Key responsibilities

  • Cyber strategy development. Support the development and roll out of cyber security strategies, capabilities and operating models that meet client goals.
  • Stakeholder management. Understand client needs, challenges and opportunities, building strong relationships as a trusted partner and ‘critical friend’.
  • Risk management. Carry out risk and maturity assessments, giving strategic guidance on improving security posture with a risk-based approach to mitigations and controls.
  • Communication. Produce presentations, reports and wider communications for diverse audiences, including the C-suite, translating technical concepts into business language.
  • Control implementation. Implement security controls, using your understanding of how they interact with IT service delivery.
  • Delivery. Deliver high quality work to agreed timelines, taking the initiative and drawing on the wider team when it helps.
  • Growth. Help support and grow Daintta by contributing to company strategy and shaping its future.
